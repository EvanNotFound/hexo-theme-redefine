# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Bloggers and readers use Redefine for publishing, browsing, and reading personal writing on Hexo sites.

## Product Purpose

Redefine provides a fast, customizable Hexo theme that makes personal writing easy to publish and comfortable to read while supporting practical writing tools and content modules.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, personal. The theme should feel clear and considered without taking attention away from the author's content.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic, over-decorated marketing layouts, excessive effects, noisy motion, and visual styling that makes the theme feel like a dashboard or obscures the author's voice.

## Design Principles

- Keep reading and writing content as the primary visual focus.
- Use precise hierarchy and alignment to make complex content easy to scan.
- Keep visual effects restrained but meaningful, using elevation to communicate structure.
- Preserve enough flexibility for each author's personal identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional requirements beyond the existing light/dark modes and keyboard interaction patterns.
